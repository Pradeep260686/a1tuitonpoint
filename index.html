FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining A1 TUTION POINT.

Which courses do you offer?

We offer IIT-JEE (Main & Advanced), NEET preparation, and Foundation courses for students from Class 6 to 12. Both online and offline modes are available.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes! We offer fully live, interactive online classes via HD video conferencing. All sessions are also recorded and available for revision anytime.

How are doubt sessions conducted?

Doubt sessions are held weekly for each subject, both online and offline. Students can also message their mentors anytime for quick help.

What is the fee structure?

Fee varies by course and batch. Please contact us on WhatsApp or email for the latest fee structure and scholarship opportunities.

Is there a free trial class available?

Yes! We offer a free demo class for new students. Contact us to schedule your free trial and experience our teaching quality firsthand.

Do you provide study materials?

Yes, all enrolled students receive comprehensive, exam-focused notes and study materials curated by our expert faculty at no extra cost.
